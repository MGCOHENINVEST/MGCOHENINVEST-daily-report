## 2. What changed since previous freeze

### 2.1 Data schema – Equities

In docs/DATA_SCHEMA_2025-11-14.md:

- Added Section 9 – Equity input tables (canonical CSV inputs), covering:

  - 9.1 prices.csv  
    - Structure: date,ticker,exchange,close,volume,currency,source  
    - Notes:
      - date = market date for close (normally T-1).
      - ticker & exchange are canonical IDs aligned with data/stock.csv.
      - close is official EOD close in currency.
      - volume optional; may be 0/null if vendor doesn’t supply.
      - source typically EODHD or a manual override label.

  - 9.2 dividends.csv  
    - Structure: ticker,exchange,ex_date,pay_date,amount,currency,source  
    - Notes:
      - amount is a decimal in currency (no "2.3p" strings).
      - ex_date / pay_date are proper ISO dates.
      - ticker/exchange consistent with data/stock.csv and prices.csv.

  - 9.3 equity_overview.csv  
    - Single row per (ticker, exchange) in the equity universe.
    - Columns (one line):  
      ticker,exchange,isin,name,country,sector,currency,  
      price_today,price_d1,price_w1,price_m1,price_m3,price_m6,price_y1,  
      ret_d1,ret_w1,ret_m1,ret_m3,ret_m6,ret_y1,  
      hi_52w,lo_52w,drawdown_from_hi_pct,  
      div_trailing_12m,div_yield_trailing_12m,  
      next_div_ex_date,next_div_pay_date,next_div_amount,next_div_currency,  
      notes_flag,watchlist_flag
    - Returns are trailing:
      - d1 ≈ 1 trading day
      - w1 ≈ 5 trading days
      - m1 ≈ 21 trading days
      - m3 ≈ 63 trading days
      - m6 ≈ 126 trading days
      - y1 ≈ 252 (trailing 12 months, not calendar YTD)
    - div_trailing_12m = sum of cash dividends over trailing 12 months.
    - div_yield_trailing_12m = div_trailing_12m / price_today.
    - Flags:
      - notes_flag for annotations like "NEW_HIGH", "EX_DIV_TODAY", "WATCH".
      - watchlist_flag boolean (0/1) tagging watchlist / focus names.

General:
- All equity joins are explicitly (ticker, exchange) consistent with data/stock.csv.
- Lookbacks are trailing windows, not YTD. This is now baked into the schema.
